MINECRAFT • SURVIVAL

From first night to the End.

A simple progression path so you always know what is worth doing next.

01

First day

Collect wood, stone, food, coal, and basic iron. Make a bed and secure a shelter before night.

02

Gear up

Build an iron set, shield, bucket, tools, and a reliable food supply. Start mining deeper for better resources.

03

Enchant

Set up bookshelves, an enchanting table, and a way to collect experience. Prioritize useful durability and protection upgrades.

04

Nether

Bring strong armor, food, blocks, and a way home. Mark your portal coordinates and avoid carrying everything valuable at once.

05

Prepare

Upgrade gear, stock food and blocks, and gather the resources needed for your next objective. Keep backups at your base.

06

The End

When you are ready, bring ranged weapons, building blocks, food, strong armor, and a plan for dealing with the dragon and Endermen.

Beginner rules that save worlds

  • Keep a backup of valuable items before risky trips.
  • Write down important coordinates.
  • Carry a water bucket when exploring the Overworld.
  • Do not dig straight down or straight up without checking what is around you.
  • Use a safe, well-lit storage area and organize important resources.
